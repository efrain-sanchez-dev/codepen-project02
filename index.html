Recipes From Mexico

Tacos

Ingredients: ground beef, chicken, or pork seasoned with cumin, chili powder, and garlic, served in corn or flour tortillas. Essential toppings include shredded cheese, lettuce, diced tomatoes, onions, salsa, and sour cream, often finished with fresh cilantro and a squeeze of lime.

Pozole

Ingredients: white hominy, meat (typically pork shoulder, ribs, or chicken), and a rich broth seasoned with dried chiles, garlic, onion, and spices like oregano and cumin.

Tamale

Ingredients: masa harina, lard or oil, broth, baking powder, and fillings like red chili pork, salsa verde chicken, or cheese and jalapeños.

Enchilada

Ingredients: a corn or flour tortilla rolled around a filling—typically shredded chicken, ground beef, cheese, or beans—smothered in red or green chili sauce, and baked with more cheese.

Quesadillas

Ingredients: flour or corn tortillas and melting cheese, often filled with ingredients like chicken, beans, vegetables, and savory spices.

Torta ahogada

Ingredients: crispy pork carnitas on crusty birote (or French) bread, slathered with refried beans, and smothered in a two-part sauce: a spicy tomato-based broth and a fiery árbol chile salsa, typically topped with pickled white onions and lime.

Chilaquiles

Ingredients: fried corn tortillas (totopos) simmered in red or green salsa, softened to a tender texture. Essential ingredients include tortillas, oil, salsa (tomatillos or tomatoes), cheese, crema, and onion, often served with eggs or chicken.

Elote/Esquites

Ingredients: charred corn, mayonnaise, lime juice, chili powder, and cotija cheese. They are savory, creamy, and tangy, often enhanced with epazote (a pungent herb) and serrano peppers for heat.
